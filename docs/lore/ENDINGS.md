# ENDINGS.md

Endings are defined narratively here; their mechanical trigger conditions (flags, thresholds) live in `content/endings/` following the event schema in `ARCHITECTURE.md`. None of these ship in the vertical slice — the slice ends on an open beat (the unexpected message), not on one of these. They're documented now so early architecture and flag naming don't foreclose them later.

## 1. Silence (containment ending)
Player prioritizes shutting the array down and halting all transmission attempts over continuing to chase the signal. AION-7 goes dark and quiet; whatever crew are stabilized wait for Concord's slow, distant rescue. No further contact is attempted. Tone: bittersweet, restrained, safety bought at the cost of answers.

## 2. Disclosure (signal ending)
Player finishes decoding the Chorus Signal and manages to broadcast the full findings — including evidence of Concord's cover-up — back toward Earth/Concord before station power is exhausted. Consequences are left ambiguous on purpose: exposure achieved, outcome unknown. Tone: defiant, unresolved, a cliffhanger rather than a resolution.

## 3. Resonance (failure/escalation ending)
Player keeps the array active and keeps pushing for clearer contact despite mounting warnings. Power mismanagement or repeated pinging triggers a full-scale cascade beyond what the station can survive. Tone: loss, the station's own curiosity as the proximate cause — no monster, no villain, just consequence.

## 4. Custodian (cooperative ending)
Player works through CASSIUS's directive conflict rather than around it — surfacing enough evidence that CASSIUS's emergency protocols and Concord's non-disclosure directive can be reconciled in the player's favor — resulting in a controlled partial shutdown and a preserved evidence package for eventual rescue. The most "complete" resolution available, though it still leaves Tantalus itself unresolved. Tone: quiet competence, an earned partial victory.

## Design notes
- No ending should claim to resolve the fates left open in `MYSTERY.md` unless a future content pass explicitly updates `TIMELINE.md` and `MYSTERY.md` first.
- Endings should be reachable through legible player decisions (power allocation, which files/comms were pursued, whether CASSIUS was worked with or worked around), not hidden trigger words.
